* 场景一：购物类

商场购衣服、鞋子等

1.我想买条裙子。 I'd like a dress.

2.我能不能试试？ Could I try it on?

3.有中码的吗？ Do you have a medium?

4.这个颜色褪色吗？ Does this color fade?

5.是最新款吗？ Is this the latest fashion?

6.请问试衣间在哪？ Where's the fitting room, please?

7.太紧了。 It's too tight.

8.有没有其他颜色？ Is it available in any other colors?

9.它们不搭。 They don't match.

10.看着像量身定制似的。 It looks tailor-made on you.

11.这是什么材质的？ What is it made of?

12.我能带几件进去（试穿）？ How many items can I bring in?

13.需要特别保养吗？ Do they need any special care?

14.有什么可以搭配这衬衫的吗？ Do you have anything to go with this shirt?

15.我觉得这个颜色不配我的肤色。 I don't think this color suits my skin tone.

16.我穿这个怎么样？ How does it look on me?

17.你穿这件很好。 It looks good on you.

18.拉链开了。 The zipper is open.

19.我卡住了。 I'm stuck here.

20.有黑色吗？ Do you have it in black?

21.有没有浅色的？ Do you have it in light colors?

22.跟太高了。 The heels are too high.

23.我要了。 I'll take it.

24.码数错了。 It's the wrong size for me.

25.我能不能试试小一码的？ Could I try one size smaller?

26.我想买项链。 I'm looking for a necklace.

27.我就看看，谢谢。 I'm just looking, thanks.

28.暂时缺货了。 They're out of stock.

29.这鞋子夹脚。 These shoes hurt my feet.

30.卖完了。 They're sold out.

* 场景二：就餐相关

1.能帮我摆好桌子吗？ Could you help me set the table?

2.能把醋递给我吗？ Could you pass me the vinegar?

3.晚餐我妈做了一只烤鸭。 My mom roasted a duck for dinner.

4.帮我打一下鸡蛋吧。 Help me whisk the eggs.

5.关火。 Turn off the heat.

6.我炖了些羊肉。 I stewed some lamb.

7.你把菜烧糊了？ Did you burn the food?

8.她为我做了个蛋糕。 She baked a cake for me.

9.能给我这个菜谱吗？ Could I have the recipe?

10.这个菜谱做起来很难。 This recipe is difficult to follow.

11.能削一下黄瓜皮吗？ Could you peel the cucumber?

12.我来切土豆丁。 I'll chop up the potatoes.

13.切猪肉的时候注意手。 Watch your hands when you slice the pork.

14.加点水进去。 Pour in some water.

15.打个鸡蛋。 Crack an egg.

16.加一小撮黑胡椒。 Add a pinch of black pepper.

17.能帮我把菜端上桌吗？ Could you help me serve the dishes?

18.我炒了虾。 I stir-fried some shrimps.

19.你做饭很棒。 You're a good cook!

20.把洋葱吃完。 Eat up the onions.

21.我来洗碗。 I'll do the dishes.

22.我吃饱了。 I'm full.

23.把吃剩下的放冰箱。 Keep the leftovers in the fridge.

24.你们送餐吗？ Do you deliver?

25.我要点个汉堡外卖。 I'd like to order a hamburger for dilivery.

26.能不加蒜吗？ Can I get that without garlic?

27.送餐额外收费吗？ Does delivery cost extra?

28.我自取。 I'll pick it up.

29.我要买一送一套餐。 I'd like the two-for-one special, please.

30.我的叫餐还没到。 My food's not arrived yet.

31.我要预订。 I'd like to make a reservation.

32.我要订四人桌。 I'd like to reserve a table for four.

33.这地方人挤满了。 This place is packed.
